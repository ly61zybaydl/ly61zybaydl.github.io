About

📍 Wuhan, China

Hi! I'm Yi Liu (刘亿), a fourth-year Artificial Intelligence undergraduate at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). I'm curious about how large language models can reason efficiently — retrieving only when they have to, improving from their own feedback, and acting in the world through tools.

I'm a senior member of the ML · NLP · Multimodal group at One Lab (HUST, advised by Prof. Yao Wan) and a remote research intern in Prof. Furong Huang's lab at the University of Maryland. In Fall 2027 I will begin my master's studies at the School of Computer Science and Technology, HUST, under the supervision of Prof. Yao Wan.

Outside research, I'm trying to be a person who keeps searching for fun — still working on it.

Publications & Projects

AAAI 2027 · Under review

Task-Aware Routing for Efficient Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Long-Context Reasoning in LLMs

Yi Liu, Yao Wan

ActionRAG uses a router to adaptively choose among five RAG execution strategies based on query complexity — cutting unnecessary retrieval on simple questions while reserving stronger knowledge augmentation for complex, long-context reasoning.

First authorPreprint coming soon
KDD 2025

LaTCoder: Converting Webpage Design to Code with Layout-as-Thought

Yi Gui, Zhen Li, Zhongyi Zhang, Guohao Wang, Tianpeng Lv, Gaoyang Jiang, Yi Liu, Dongping Chen, Yao Wan, Hongyu Zhang, Wenbin Jiang, Xuanhua Shi, Hai Jin

Developed an automated layout-segmentation scheme with large multimodal models, built the Layout-to-Code baseline and evaluation pipeline, and tuned in-context-learning and chain-of-thought prompting for more robust, structurally consistent HTML/CSS.

Co-author PDF DOI
Open source

Word2Xmind LocalLLM: Turning Word Requirements into XMind Test-Case Mind Maps with a Local LLM

Yi Liu · Python · MIT license

A tool for QA and test engineers that converts structured Word requirement documents into XMind-compatible test-case mind maps. It runs models locally through Ollama for a 100% on-premise, privacy-preserving workflow, with OrcaRouter available as an opt-in hosted backend. Invited for inclusion by the OrcaRouter gateway and for release and iteration on Reef by the Human-Agent Society.

Author & maintainer GitHub Reef
